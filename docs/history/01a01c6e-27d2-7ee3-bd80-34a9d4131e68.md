## 2026-08-19T23:51:01.357Z — assistant

Yes. EllesmereUI is flagging gear that needs attention:

- The small flashing red pentagon means **missing enchant**. Hover it and the tooltip should say “Enchant missing.”
- A red gear-slot border can also indicate a **missing enchant or an empty gem socket**, depending on the slot.

Once you enchant the eligible pieces and fill any empty sockets, the warnings should disappear. If you only want to hide them, go to **EllesmereUI → Blizzard UI Enhanced → Character Sheet** and disable **Show Enchants** and/or **Show Gems**.
