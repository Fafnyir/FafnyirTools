Fafnyir Tools for EllesmereUI v1.1.4 — WoW Forever beta

Added
- WoW Forever interface 16001 support and runtime detection.
- Forever-only Volumetric Fog toggle under QoL.
- Current Pet Journal owned-ID enumeration with the legacy fallback retained.

Removed
- Unit Frame Sources, Aura Skins, and Resting; EllesmereUI now owns those options.
- The Unit Frames page. /faftools now opens QoL.
- Removed-feature settings from new global exports. Existing SavedVariables remain untouched and inactive.

Changed
- Settings import/restore and Full Reset are guarded while EllesmereUI reports
  the Forever beta SavedVariables defect.

Persistence
- Volumetric Fog directly controls the client volumeFog CVar. The client
  persists it independently of addon SavedVariables.
- Export remains available during the beta persistence defect.

Scope
This build is offline-validated for WoW Forever beta 1.60.1 and EllesmereUI
9.2.1. Full in-game regression testing remains deferred until Blizzard fixes
the SavedVariables defect. The accepted Retail release remains v1.1.3.
